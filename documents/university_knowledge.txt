UNIVERSITY KNOWLEDGE

1.  Admissions Students can apply for undergraduate and postgraduate
    programs through the university admission process.

2.  Courses The university offers programs in Computer Science, Business
    Administration, Data Science and Engineering.

3.  Examinations Students must attend internal assessments and semester
    examinations.

4.  Library The university library provides textbooks, journals and
    digital learning resources.

5.  Attendance Students are expected to maintain at least 75 percent
    attendance to be eligible for examinations.
